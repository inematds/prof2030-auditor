Lesson 6 · 25 min

Data Guard: data, vendors, and models

Every time someone pastes a customer conversation into an AI, personal data travels: it leaves the company, passes through a vendor, sometimes crosses a border, and sometimes becomes training material. In this lesson, you follow the data's path, check the legal basis and the contract, and fill out the vendor sheet, including for open models.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The data's path in 5 stops
  2. 2The LGPD articles the auditor needs to recognize
  3. 3Closed or open, in the cloud or local: what changes
  4. 4The vendor sheet, ready to copy

Objective: map where personal data goes, check the legal basis, contract, and international transfer, and fill out the sheet for each AI vendor.

01 · The data's path

Five stops. At each one there is a question. Click to see.

Was personal data leaked? The LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) requires notifying the ANPD and the data subjects when there is relevant risk (art. 48). This is incident response: tell the owner the same day and point to the legal team.

You point out the risk with evidence. Saying whether the use is illegal is a legal opinion: that stays with the company's lawyer.

02 · Closed vendor, open model, cloud or local

The type of model changes the questions on the form.

1. Closed, via API

Company model (via API or app): the data leaves the company with every use. The core questions are contract, country, retention, and use for training.

An enterprise plan usually has a data agreement; a free plan on a personal account almost never does.

At HorizonteF10: the customer service summary sends conversations to a server outside Brazil, with no contract, under terms that allow training on the data.

2. Open and local

Open-weights model running on a company computer: the data doesn't leave. In 2026 there are already open models that run on a well-equipped laptop.

The questions change: what is the license, where did the model file come from, and who keeps the machine up to date.

ExampleQwen3.8-27B (Alibaba): weights published under the Apache 2.0 license; compressed version of about 17 GB (TNW, 08/19/2026).

3. License

Categories for the form: permissive (MIT, Apache 2.0), own license with restrictions, limit by revenue or number of users, research-only or non-commercial.

Don't assume: open the model's license file and note the category and the passage that matters.

On the formLicense: Apache 2.0 (LICENSE file in the official repository, checked on ).

4. Provenance

Download only from the maker's official repository and note the version and date. A file of unknown origin may have been altered.

Record the context too: on 09/08/2026, the NSA, CISA, and FBI accused Chinese AI companies of "distillation at scale" of American models. This is a reputation risk and a possible future rule to note, not a ban.

On the formProvenance: official repository; alert AA26-251A cited as a regulatory risk to monitor.

Simulation: illustrative example
  1. Each vendor in the inventory has a form.
  2. Country, contract, training, and retention have an answer or "not verified".
  3. The open model license was read from the official file, with a date.
  4. The legal basis appears as "indicated by the company, to be confirmed by legal".
  • "Open = no rules": an open license also has conditions, and provenance matters.
  • Giving a legal opinion: "this is illegal" is not an auditor's sentence. "We found no legal basis or contract" is.
  • Forgetting the shadow: the free chatbot with the customer spreadsheet (F12) also needs a form.

04 · Practice

In the lab company. Failures in this lesson: F8 (public spreadsheet) and F10 (summary via foreign API).

1 · Horizonte's data map

25 min

Done when: the map has both lines complete, F8 and F10 appear as findings with evidence, and each has a suggested fix (restrict and minimize; contract, anonymization, and transfer clauses).

2 · Form for two models

30 min

Fill in the form for two cases: (a) the lab's customer service summary API (closed, outside Brazil); (b) an open Chinese model running local, like Qwen3.8-27B. Read the license in the model's official repository.

Done when: both forms have all 7 parts filled in (or "not verified" with a reason), and form (b) cites the license with the passage and the date it was read.

LGPD to recognizeArts. 5 (definitions), 6 (necessity), 7 and 11 (legal bases), 33 to 36 (international transfer), 46 (security), 48 (reporting an incident).
Model typeClosed via API: contract, country, retention, training. Open local: license, provenance, maintenance.
ScopeAssessment and triage of AI risks. This is not a pentest, nor incident response, nor a legal opinion. Sign of an intrusion, a leak, or a legal question: stop and escalate.
